    What do I need at check-in?

    A valid government photo ID for every adult guest. Check-in is private and contactless, and the front desk is open around the clock if you need help.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, free private parking is available on site. Tell the front desk about your vehicle when you enquire.

    Can I cook in the apartments?

    The apartments have a kitchen. You can also ask about room service, food delivery and takeaway breakfast.

    Are children and pets welcome?

    Children of all ages are welcome, and cribs are available in the double and two-bedroom rooms. Pets are not accepted.

    Is smoking allowed?

    The property is non-smoking. Parties such as hen and stag events are not permitted.

    What time is check-in and check-out?

    Times to be confirmed with the property. Early arrival and late departure can be requested at the front desk, subject to availability.
